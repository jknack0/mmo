# Discipline skill design template: 12 / 1·3·3·2·1·2 / 6-slot / tripod T1·T2 / 20-node tree

Every discipline ships **12 skills** with a fixed anatomy: **1 mobility, 3 spammable (low Spirit, ≤5s cd), 3 mid-cd burst (med Spirit, ~8–12s cd), 2 heavy nuke (high Spirit, 25–45s cd), 1 utility (CC/buff/debuff), 2 elite (Wrath-costed)**. Players slot **6 of 24** available skills onto the hotbar with **free distribution** across their two equipped disciplines — 6+0, 3+3, 5+1, anything goes. Each skill has a tripod with **Tier 1 = vector tradeoff** (damage vs range vs radius vs Burn-apply vs cd) and **Tier 2 = archetype lean** (one [B]urn / one [D]irect-burst / one [U]tility option per skill, where the discipline's archetypes are defined per-discipline). Each discipline has a **20-node passive tree**: 2 shared root nodes + 3 archetype-aligned paths of 6 nodes each (3 boosts → 1–2 synergies → 1 keystone). Players share a **single pool of ~20 passive points** across both discipline trees — enough to reach 1 keystone comfortably (8 nodes: 2 root + 6 path), 2 keystones with sacrifice (14 nodes total when sharing roots), and never 3 cleanly. The reason for this strictness is solo-dev tractability and player legibility: the same anatomy across all 6 disciplines means a player learns the rhythm once and applies it everywhere; the same tripod schema (T1=vector, T2=archetype) means tripod choice is always meaningful and pattern-readable; the same tree shape means passive planning is portable knowledge. The reason for **free** skill distribution rather than forced split is that the classless contract from ADR-0004 only holds if a player can lean fully into either discipline — and that forces every skill to stand on its own merit rather than relying on combo guarantees. The reason for **20 points across both trees** (not per-tree) is to make the cross-discipline trade-off real: every passive point spent in Pyromancy is one not spent in Blademaster. This template is what Pyromancy (the worked example in [`/docs/disciplines/pyromancy.md`](../disciplines/pyromancy.md)) demonstrates; the remaining 5 disciplines (Cryomancy, Blademaster, Marksman, and 2 TBD) follow the same scaffolding with their own thematic skills and per-discipline archetype leans.
