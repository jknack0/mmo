# Two-resource system: Spirit (fast, regenerating) + Wrath (slow, combat-built)

Combat uses two universal resources rather than one or per-discipline resources. **Spirit** regenerates over time and from right-click attacks, fueling moment-to-moment skills. **Wrath** builds only from dealing damage, decays out of combat, and fuels 1–2 elite skills per discipline. Most skills cost Spirit; a few elite skills cost Wrath; a small number cost both. The reason for two rather than one is texture: a single resource gives every fight one rhythm; two layers Spirit management (moment-to-moment) under Wrath anticipation (set up the burst), preserving the spirit of Lost Ark's class identity gauges without binding flavor to a specific class. Universal names with keystone mutations preserve the classless contract — a Pyromancer+Blademaster has a coherent resource model whether casting Pyro or Blade skills. Keystones in the passive tree can mutate either resource (e.g., "your Wrath builds from damage taken instead of damage dealt"), which is where build-defining flavor lives without proliferating resource types. Per-discipline named resources (mana, fury, etc.) were explicitly rejected because they break the classless mixing model — combining a "rage" discipline with a "mana" discipline produces a UI and balance nightmare.
