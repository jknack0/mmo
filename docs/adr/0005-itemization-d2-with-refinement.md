# D2-style itemization with Lost Ark-style item tapping

Itemization follows the D2 model: White → Magic → Rare → Set → Unique tiers, with affixes split ~60% stat / ~40% skill, named uniques as fixed-stat chase items, and Magic Find as a character-level statistic rather than a gear affix (to prevent the D2 wart of MF outfits supplanting combat outfits). Players can additionally **tap** items to raise their **Refinement** level (+0 to +10), multiplying all numeric stats on the item. Tapping failure burns materials but never destroys the item — a deliberate departure from BDO; for a browser semi-hardcore game, destruction is rage-quit fuel. Refinement caps are bounded by base item tier so a tapped white item cannot obsolete a rare. The composite loop — find good base → roll good affixes → tap for Refinement — gives a single item three progression phases, addressing D2's lack of "invest in this specific item" depth. Player trading uses both in-person trade windows (D2-flavored social haggling) and an auction house (modern liquidity expectation). Tier inflation patches are explicitly rejected; new content adds new affixes and chase items, never new tiers of the same item.
