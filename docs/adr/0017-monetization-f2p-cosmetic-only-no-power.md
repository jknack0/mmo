# Monetization: F2P at launch, cosmetic-only cash shop, no power/convenience/time saves

Game is free during alpha and beta with no payments accepted. At launch it goes **free-to-play** with a **cosmetic-only cash shop** — character skins, weapon/armor transmogs, mount/pet skins, dyes, emotes, name change, character slot expansion (above a generous free baseline). An **optional Patreon** exists for players who want to support development; Patreon tiers grant only cosmetic flair (forum badge, exclusive cosmetic drops on a delay) and **never** in-game power. Hard nos, locked: **no power sales** (no gear, no stats, no XP boosts, no skill unlocks), **no convenience sales** (no inventory tabs that gate hoarding, no auction-house slot expansion, no faster mounts, no rested-XP boosters, no respec tokens), **no time saves** (no daily-skip tokens, no instant cooldowns, no catchup purchases — Vigor catchup from ADR-0007 is the only catchup mechanism and it's free), **no loot boxes / gacha** in any form (including "mystery cosmetics"), **no subscriptions**, **no battle pass / season pass**. The single in-game gold sink at launch is the **auction-house 5% gold fee on completed sales** (ADR-0005 already names the AH; this locks the rate). The reason for the strict separation is that classless 2-discipline (ADR-0004) plus D2 itemization (ADR-0005) plus no-dailies endgame (ADR-0007) only stay coherent if power and time are never for sale — the moment a cash shop sells stats or skips, the gear chase and endgame pillars collapse into pay-to-win and the design loses its identity. Cosmetic-only is also the **only** model where a solo dev can ship updates on a sustainable cadence without the live-ops treadmill that battle passes and seasonal monetization demand. **Reserved, not committed:** future cosmetic-only seasonal events (paid event cosmetics, never event power); a possible founder's-pack at launch (cosmetic bundle + name reservation + early access window, never stats). Anything sold for real money must pass the test: *would removing this from the shop affect any player's combat outcomes or progression speed?* If yes, it doesn't ship.
