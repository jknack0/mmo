# PvP deferred indefinitely; PvE-first

No PvP at launch in any form (no dueling, no arena, no world PvP). The deferral is deliberate, not an oversight. The classless 2-discipline system makes PvP balance combinatorially hard — 6 disciplines yield 15 archetype combinations, each tunable via tripods and passive tree, and balancing that surface for PvP independent of PvE is real work. World PvP would additionally be grief-vulnerable in a shared-world architecture and would actively hurt the "play together casually" feel of open zones. The hard no on world PvP is permanent; arena/dueling are reserved for far-post-launch consideration once the PvE meta has matured. The genre proves PvE-first is fine: PoE is fanatically loved with effectively zero PvP, and Lost Ark's PvP is its weakest pillar.
