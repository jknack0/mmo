# Three-pillar endgame: Rifts, Trials, World Activities — with Vigor catchup, no dailies

The endgame is three pillars: **Rifts** (instanced solo/PUG content, ~10–15 min, two-phase wave-clear-then-mini-boss in the Lost Ark Chaos+Guardian mold), **Trials** (scheduled group raids with hard mechanics, weekly best-reward lockout), and **World Activities** (open-zone shared content that justifies the shared-world architecture). Daily quests are explicitly rejected — the #1 cited burnout reason from ex-Lost-Ark players. The retention tension is instead resolved with **Vigor**: a per-character catchup currency that accumulates while offline or doing non-Rift content (caps at 7 days), spent on Rift runs for bonus rewards. A player who logs in weekly burns 7 Vigor in one session and roughly matches a daily grinder; the daily grinder gets 365 bonus runs/year. Both feel rewarded, neither punished. We accept the cost: aggregate engagement-hours-per-player will be lower than a daily-driven loop, because daily obligation generates "compulsion play" that we're declining to harvest. This is a values choice favoring long-term goodwill over engagement metrics.
