# Setting: post-Sundering dying world (Vael), Awakened players, Veil-as-endgame-escalator

The world is **Vael** (tentative name). ~30 years ago, the **Sundering** — a reality-warping catastrophe — shattered the southern continent and tore holes in the **Veil** between the mortal realm and what lies beneath. Two-thirds of the population was lost. Survivors fled north to fortified **Holds**; the abandoned south is the **Wastes**, claimed by corruption. The launch playable Hold is **Hold Veridian**, the central social/trading hub where all 6 discipline trainers reside. Players are **the Awakened** — a generation born post-Sundering with **the Mark**, the latent ability to learn any combination of magical disciplines. This is the in-world justification for the classless 2-discipline system; without it, the gameplay system has no lore hook. High-tier endgame (top-tier Rifts and Trials) happens *across the Veil* in adjacent realms (the Hunger, the Ashen Sea, the Wormhouse), which gives lore-permission for any aesthetic at high tier and lets future content patches introduce new realms without rewriting Vael itself. Tone is terse, weary, earnest — D2-flavored, not Lost Ark-warm; NPCs have all lost someone. No active gods (most died in the Sundering), only residue: divine relics in the wastes, prayer-fragments etched in ruins. The deliberate departure from both references is mood: Lost Ark's setting is grimdark-in-backstory but warm-in-moment; ours is grimdark all the way down. The cost is some player bounce ("I want a friendlier ARPG"); the gain is an identity that's recognizable shorthand without being a clone of Dark Souls or D2.
