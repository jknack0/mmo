# Four D2-named stats, no level-up allocation

Characters have four primary stats — Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Vitality — using the D2 names that players already understand. Stats are sourced entirely from gear, passive tree, and discipline level; there is no manual point allocation on level-up. The deliberate rejection of level-up allocation is the surprising part: D2 nostalgia and PoE both have it, and its absence will be noticed. The reason is that D2's stat allocation was agency theatre — every point felt important, but in practice nearly all points went into VIT after meeting gear requirements, making the entire system shallow despite its appearance. Removing allocation kills the "wasted a stat point" rage and the "everyone allocates the same" trap in one move. Build agency moves entirely to gear choice, damage-type affix collection, and passive tree pathing — all of which have richer decision spaces. Damage-type affixes (+Fire damage, +Bleed damage) are the primary build-defining item affix, not raw stats.
