# Social systems: 2026 modern bar at launch, Orders ship guild-light

The launch social bar is the modern MMO minimum: party, friends list (per-account, online status, private messages), mailbox (per-account, durable, items expire 30 days), block/mute (per-account, 500-cap), and a multi-channel chat system (Zone, Global rate-limited, Trade rate-limited, Party, Order, /tell). **Party finder** is included and load-bearing — it's a searchable cross-channel LFG list that warps joiners to the party leader's channel, and without it solo players can't access group content. **Orders** (the setting-appropriate name for guilds) ship intentionally light: name, 4-char tag, 100 members, three ranks, recruitment notes, Order chat. Order banks, halls, quests, and Order-vs-Order are explicitly deferred to post-launch content patches. The reason for the split bar — modern-minimum on everything else, guild-light on Orders — is that the modern social minimum is now table stakes (shipping without it is shipping a 2005 MMO), while guild *content* is the single biggest scope-creep magnet in MMO design. Shipping with a working basic Order structure means players can self-organize socially without committing the solo dev to authoring a whole second content vertical. **All social state lives per-account, not per-character** — friends, blocks, mail, Order membership all persist across alts and seasonal characters. The only per-character thing is the active party. This pulls naturally on the existing Redis-pub/sub infrastructure for cross-channel chat (ADR-0012) and Postgres for durable mail (ADR-0013), so the architecture cost is small.
